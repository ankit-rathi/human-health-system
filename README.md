# The Human Health System

**Understand. Improve. Sustain.**

An evidence-informed health education project that makes the science of
human health understandable, visual and actionable.

The goal is to help everyday people understand how the human body works,
how lifestyle factors interact, how to evaluate health information, and
how to make informed, sustainable decisions.

This repository is the project's source of truth: its philosophy,
curriculum, teaching method, evidence standards, professional boundaries
and development roadmap.

## What we are building

An education-first platform that may eventually include:

-   A foundational book and visual guides
-   A structured learning journey and self-paced courses
-   Short videos, articles and visual explainers
-   Workshops and a learning community
-   Educational tools for understanding health measurements and tracking
    habits
-   Collaborations with qualified healthcare and fitness professionals

This is not intended to be a medical practice, a diagnostic product or a
substitute for professional care.

## Core philosophy

> Health is not a collection of isolated tips. It is an interconnected
> system that people can learn to understand, care for and improve.

We aim to make people more capable of understanding their health---not
dependent on a personality, product, trend or programme.

## How to use this repository

1.  Treat `VISION.md` as the north star.
2.  Use `CURRICULUM.md` to decide what belongs in the learning journey.
3.  Use `LEARNING-METHOD.md` whenever creating a capsule, article,
    lesson or video.
4.  Apply `EVIDENCE-AND-SCOPE.md` before publishing health claims or
    practical recommendations.
5.  Use `ROADMAP.md` to keep the project focused and sustainable.

## Working principles

-   Start with first principles; introduce jargon only when it helps.
-   Explain mechanisms, not just instructions.
-   Prefer connected mental models over disconnected lists of tips.
-   Make visuals clarify ideas rather than decorate them.
-   Separate established evidence, emerging evidence, tradition,
    hypotheses and personal experience.
-   Be transparent about uncertainty and limitations.
-   Do not diagnose, prescribe, promise cures or replace qualified
    professionals.
-   Build trust and educational value before monetisation.

## Project status

**Stage:** Foundation and pilot design.

The first milestone is not launching a company or building an app. It is
creating a small, excellent, evidence-informed learning library and
testing whether ordinary people understand and benefit from it.
