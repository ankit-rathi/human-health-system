# The Human Health System

**Understand. Improve. Sustain.**

An evidence-informed health education project that makes human health understandable, visual and actionable.

The aim is to help everyday people move from disconnected health tips to a coherent understanding of the body, the factors that influence it, the measurements used to assess it, and the decisions people make about their health.

This repository is the project's source of truth. It defines the philosophy, learning journey, teaching method, evidence standards, professional boundaries and development path.

## What we are building

An education-first platform that may grow into:

- A foundational book and visual guides.
- A structured learning journey and self-paced courses.
- Short videos, articles and visual explainers.
- Workshops and a learning community.
- General health-literacy tools for understanding measurements and habits.
- Collaborations with qualified healthcare, nutrition, movement and mental-health professionals.

It is not a medical practice, diagnostic service or substitute for professional care.

## The organising idea

> Health is not a collection of isolated numbers, habits or body parts. It is a living system: the body responds to its biology, behaviours and environment over time.

The learning journey follows this chain:

**Understand health → Understand the body → Understand its processes → Understand the influences → Connect the system → Make informed decisions → Learn and adapt.**

This is a teaching sequence, not a claim that health follows a simple linear pathway.

## Working principles

- Explain normal physiology before discussing dysfunction or disease.
- Teach mechanisms without pretending that one mechanism explains every condition.
- Connect nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, relationships and environment.
- Distinguish biological plausibility, association, intervention evidence and clinical guidance.
- Use measurements as clues, not as complete explanations.
- Make visuals clarify relationships and processes.
- Be practical without prescribing individual treatment.
- Respect the role of qualified healthcare professionals.
- Build educational trust before monetisation.

## Current stage

**Stage: Foundation and learning-architecture design.**

The next milestone is to map the first 30 capsules, show their prerequisites and cross-links, and create a small set of carefully researched pilot capsules. The objective is not to launch an app or a health programme prematurely. It is to prove that the learning method makes complex health concepts clearer.
